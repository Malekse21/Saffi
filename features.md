# Saffi System Features

## 1. Core Queue Management (Doctor/Secretary Dashboard)
- **Smart Queue**: Efficiently manage patient flow with "Walk-in", "Appointment", and "Emergency" priority levels.
- **One-Click Actions**: Call next patient, mark as away, or complete consultation with a single click.
- **Offline-First Resilience**: continue working (add/update patients) even if the internet goes down. Actions sync automatically when connection returns.
- **Status Tracking**: Visual indicators for patients who are "Waiting", "In Consultation", "Away", or "Completed".

## 2. Connected TV Display (Waiting Room)
- **Real-Time Visualization**: Auto-updating display for the waiting room showing the current patient and the upcoming queue.
- **Audio Announcements**: Integrated Text-to-Speech (TTS) voice announcements ("Ding-dong, Patient A1 to cabinet please") to alert patients.
- **Privacy Mode**: Option to hide patient names and show only ticket numbers for confidentiality.
- **Ads/Announcements Space**: Dedicated area on screen to display clinic information or "Tip of the Day".
- **Offline Reliability**: The TV screen caches the last known list and continues displaying it even without WiFi.

## 3. Patient Client Portal (Mobile)
- **Remote Tracking**: Patients can scan a QR code to track their position in the queue from their phone (no need to crowd the waiting room).
- **"Your Turn" Alerts**: Visual indicators on their phone when it's their turn.
- **Appointment Booking**: Integrated flow for scheduling future visits.

## 4. QR Station & Kiosk
- **Self-Check-In Support**: Generate QR codes for patients to scan and join the queue or track status.
- **Scanner Integration**: Built-in QR scanner for quick patient retrieval or verification.

## 5. Practice Management Tools
- **Calendar & Appointments**: Drag-and-drop calendar for scheduling and managing rendez-vous.
- **Analytics Dashboard**: Insights into patient volume, peak hours, and wait times to optimize practice flow.
- **SMS System**: Integrated SMS top-up and notification system for appointment reminders (reduces no-shows).
- **Profile Customization**: Customizable clinic branding for the TV and patient portal (Logo, Clinic Name).

## 6. Technical Advantages
- **PWA (Progressive Web App)**: Installable on phones and desktops like a native app.
- **Secure Authentication**: Robust user management and access control.
- **Cloud Sync**: Real-time synchronization across all devices (Secretary PC, Doctor PC, TV, Patient Mobile).
